## Getting Started

InstaPulse is an emergency alert and rapid response system designed to keep you and your loved ones safe. To get started, you'll need to create an account and complete the verification process.

## Account Registration

### Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Visit the InstaPulse website
2. Click the "Register" button in the navigation
3. Fill in the required information:
   - Email address
   - Password
   - Full name
   - Phone number
4. Click "Create Account"

### Step 2: Verify Your Email

After registration, you'll receive a verification email. Click the link in the email to verify your account.

### Step 3: Complete Your Profile

Once logged in, complete your profile information including:
- Address
- Emergency contacts
- Additional personal details

## KYC Verification

To ensure security and prevent fraud, all users must complete Know Your Customer (KYC) verification.

### Required Documents

You'll need to provide:
- Valid government-issued ID (front and back)
- Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement, etc.)
- Selfie holding your ID

### How to Upload

1. Go to the KYC Verification page
2. Click "Upload Document" for each required document
3. Select the file from your device
4. Wait for the upload to complete
5. Submit your documents for review

### Verification Timeline

- Standard verification: 1-3 business days
- You'll receive an email notification when approved
- If rejected, you'll be informed of the reason and can resubmit

## Making a Purchase

### Step 1: Select a Package

1. Browse available packages
2. Click "Select Package" on your preferred tier
3. Review the package details

### Step 2: Checkout

1. Fill in your billing information
2. Select your preferred payment method:
   - GCash
   - Maya
   - Credit/Debit Card
   - Online Banking
3. Review your order
4. Complete payment

### Step 3: Confirmation

After successful payment:
- You'll receive a confirmation email
- Your dashboard will show your active package
- Installation will be scheduled (if applicable)

## Troubleshooting

### Login Issues

- Ensure you're using the correct email
- Reset your password if forgotten
- Check if your account is verified
- Clear your browser cache

### Payment Issues

- Verify your payment method details
- Check if you have sufficient funds
- Try a different payment method
- Contact support if issues persist

### KYC Issues

- Ensure documents are clear and readable
- Check that all information is visible
- Use valid, unexpired documents
- Contact support if verification is delayed

## Security Best Practices

1. Use a strong, unique password
2. Enable two-factor authentication when available
3. Don't share your account credentials
4. Log out after each session
5. Report suspicious activity immediately
